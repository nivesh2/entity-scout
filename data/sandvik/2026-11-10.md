- **What happened:** Sandvik's Q3 report re-confirms its four long-term financial targets for 2025-2030: 7% growth through a cycle (organic and M&A), an adjusted EBITA margin of 20-22% through a cycle, a 50% dividend payout of adjusted EPS, and financial net debt/EBITDA below 1.5 (excluding transformational M&A). Expect the 24-25 November Capital Markets Day to be the venue where Sandvik is asked to raise the margin range, set a higher growth ambition or put numbers on digital and aftermarket.
