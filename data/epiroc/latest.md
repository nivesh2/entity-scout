## Strategy & stated priorities
### [F01] Capital Markets Day reconfirms goals and recasts Epiroc as a 'productivity and technology partner' · 2026-06-08 · acceleration
- **What happened:** At its Capital Markets Day in Örebro, Epiroc reconfirmed its financial goals (8% annual revenue growth over a business cycle, industry-best operating margin, dividend of 50% of net profit over the cycle) and its near-term outlook. The CEO described a move 'from being an equipment supplier to becoming a strong productivity and technology partner'. The day covered five strategic areas: attractive niches, innovation, aftermarket, operational excellence and outperformance. It also covered automation including mixed fleet, exploration (rolling 12-month exploration orders of SEK 3.1bn, about 4.7% of Group orders, at 31 March 2026), and a multi-brand approach that pairs premium positioning with broader reach, for example the GIA brand in China.
- **Compared to before:** No new targets. The goals and the 'attractive niches, innovation, aftermarket, operational excellence' strategy are the same as in the 2025 annual report and the quarterly reports. The new emphasis is on exploration as a growth line, multi-brand reach with different value propositions (GIA) and automation of other OEMs' machines. In the 2025 annual report, the CEO noted that the exploration business grew especially strongly in H2 2025.
- **Why it might matter:** This is the reference for the strategy team. External readers will judge every later quarter against an unchanged 8% growth goal and 'industry-best margin' claim. Publicly naming a multi-brand play with different value propositions (GIA in China) tells peers and investors that Epiroc will compete beyond the premium segment, so competitors may answer with their own mid-tier offers.

### [F02] Global partnership with China's SANY Group in attachments and mining electrification · 2026-05-26 · new
- **What happened:** Epiroc signed a strategic partnership with SANY Group. It covers cross-brand packages that combine Epiroc hydraulic breakers, specialty attachments and ground engaging tools with SANY excavators and loaders. In mining, it combines Epiroc electrified equipment, charging and service with SANY's expertise in electric microgrids and green energy. The signing took place at SANY Industrial Park in Changsha and was attended by Tools & Attachments President José Manuel Sánchez.
- **Compared to before:** An OEM-level global partnership with a Chinese heavy-equipment maker has not appeared in Epiroc's 2025 annual report or its 2026 quarterly reports, so it is new to the disclosed strategy. Before this, Epiroc's China strategy was presented mainly through its own multi-brand approach (GIA). In Q3 Epiroc also highlighted that it is a 'trusted partner to Chinese mining companies as they expand and operate internationally' (the MMG order).
- **Why it might matter:** This could open a carrier-OEM channel for attachments, a segment where infrastructure demand has been weak. It also signals a stance of working with Chinese OEMs rather than only competing with them. Sandvik and other peers, and Western carrier OEMs, may read it as a shift in Epiroc's channel strategy. Watch for any exclusivity or volume disclosures.

## Financial performance

- **Why it might matter:** The margin progress supports the stated 'industry-best margin' goal. The external debate is now about cash conversion and turning backlog into revenue, plus a softer attachments/infrastructure outlook. According to an earnings-call transcript summary (headline only), management pointed to a strong copper pipeline for Q4 2026 and Q1 2027.

## Sustainability & ESG disclosures
### [F13] Transport CO2e up 17% while operational emissions fall 14% — tension with 2030 halving goal · 2026-10-28 · inconsistency
- **What happened:** Rolling 12-month CO2e from transport (comparable units) rose 17% to 135,440 tonnes (116,094). Epiroc attributes this to higher volumes, more air freight and new transport routes. Emissions from operations fell 14% to 43,822 tonnes (50,722), helped by renewable energy and efficiency measures. On 2 September, Epiroc received an EcoVadis Gold medal for the second year (top 2%), and it restated its SBTi-validated 1.5°C targets, including halving CO2e by 2030.
- **Compared to before:** Rolling 12-month transport emissions were about 116,000 tonnes in Q3 and Q4 2025, then rose to 125,000 (Q1 2026), 126,600 (Q2) and 135,400 (Q3). This rising trend contradicts the 2030 halving ambition that Epiroc restated in the same period. Operational emissions continue to improve.
- **Why it might matter:** Transport emissions are now about three times operational emissions and are moving the wrong way, partly because of air freight used to support the production ramp-up. This gives critics and ESG raters a clear inconsistency to point at, and is a logistics cost signal as well.

- **Compared to before:** Before the window, the shares rallied after a strong Q1 (+7.0% on 29 April; about SEK 218 on 20 March). In the window that momentum reversed even though margins were the strongest in the recent series. Both report-day drops followed consensus misses on orders and EBIT (Infront).
- **Why it might matter:** Epiroc underperformed the Swedish large-cap index by about 15 percentage points and Sandvik by about 6. The market is pricing in concerns about the pace of growth and cash conversion, as well as high expectations. This matters for acquisition currency and for how investors see Epiroc versus its closest listed peer.
